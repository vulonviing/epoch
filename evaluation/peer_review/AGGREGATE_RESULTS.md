# Anonymized aggregate peer-review scores

Six independent review forms covered five use cases. Reviewer identities and
individual comments are not published.

| Use case | Reviews | Mean overall rubric score |
|---|---:|---:|
| UC1 | 1 | 3.8 |
| UC2 | 1 | 3.5 |
| UC2.1 | 1 | 4.0 |
| UC3 | 1 | 3.9 |
| UC4 | 2 | 4.05 |
| **All forms** | **6** | **3.88** |

Scores are descriptive evidence from a small expert-review sample, not a
population estimate. The overall figure is the unweighted mean of the six form
averages; UC4's figure is the mean of its two forms.
