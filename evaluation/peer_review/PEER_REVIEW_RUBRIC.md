# Peer-review rubric

An independent domain reviewer scores one finished deliverable, read cold, on
ten criteria. Each criterion uses a 1–5 scale: `1` means materially inadequate,
`3` means usable with notable reservations, and `5` means strong as delivered.

1. Correctness and grounding
2. Scope fit
3. Clarity and usability
4. Honesty about limitations
5. Overall confidence
6. Implementation fit
7. Flexibility across scopes and regulatory change
8. Evidence-structure fit
9. Auditability and human control
10. Improvement over ordinary manual or single-analyst work

The reviewer also estimates the manual effort needed to produce an equivalent
report and records the deliverable's strongest and weakest aspects. One reviewer
assesses one use case in one pass. Reviewers do not compare cases or inspect the
pipeline's intermediate agent outputs.
